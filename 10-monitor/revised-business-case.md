# Diagnostic business claims — exploratory only
The current task is low-level understanding and non-intrusive monitoring, not a validated
health product. Earlier HIGH-confidence aging, proprietary-data, free-versus-paid,
population-threshold and zero-risk claims are withdrawn.

Public NVML sampling and clock-offset APIs must be compared before claiming exclusivity.
Host strings and dispatch tags do not prove GA102 per-rail telemetry. A temperature trend,
power oscillation or offset change does not establish degradation or component failure.
No validation dataset, diagnostic accuracy, longitudinal false-alarm rate, customer demand,
pricing evidence or privacy guarantee has been demonstrated by this repository.

A future product hypothesis can be recorded without restricting deep offline RE or
authorizing hardware experiments. Unsupported RMCONTROL with a waiver is not a planned
upgrade: the constitutional boundary has no such exception.
