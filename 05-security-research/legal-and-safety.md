# Safety boundary and research material
The owner's [CONSTITUTION.md](../CONSTITUTION.md) governs the project. The former bench/
waiver/recovery guidance is superseded. No firmware, driver, setting, power-state,
raw-register, injection, stress or physical intervention is authorized.

Offline reverse engineering of firmware, drivers, libraries and protocols is permitted.
Use copies and legitimate source packages, preserve provenance, and compare applicable
sources. Do not run downloaded installers/tools on the GPU host merely to inspect them.
Unknown ABI or effects mean offline analysis only; ordinary documented monitoring is the
separate fixed collector.

A read-only label, a maintenance window, a passed-through VM, a snapshot or a spare card
does not create an exception. Recovery procedures do not make an experiment risk-free.
No API or read path carries a blanket legal/contractual/zero-liability assurance here.
Licenses and authorization depend on the actual artifacts and context; do not infer
redistribution rights from this note. Firmware binaries remain out of Git.

Security claims require a specific boundary and retained evidence. No demonstrated exploit
or absence-of-vulnerability proof is claimed by the current GC6 notes.
